# Two Americas

**Live page → https://dont-shoot-the-data.github.io/two-americas/**

One month. One story, wall to wall. Eighteen verified interracial killings, barely a national mention. This is the record of the omission.

Every case is verified against charging documents or official police releases plus at least two independent outlets. Race is coded only from booking photos, official booking records, and published photographs — never from names or third-party lists. Every photo tile on the page links to the source bearing that photograph. Full methodology in the page footer.

## Structure
- `index.html` — the entire publication, self-contained
- `img/` — case photographs and coverage screenshots, reviewed by human eyes
